# THE 26

## A Connected Universe Saga

---

### About

**THE 26** is a connected universe saga spanning 26 realities. It follows Jaxo, the God of the Multiverse, and the heroes and villains who shape the fate of all existence.

This is not one story. It is many. Different worlds. Different times. Different realities. All connected by a single thread: the anchor. The constant. The origin.

Jaxo.

### The Watcher

Jaxo is the God of the Multiverse. He is not a creator. He is not a destroyer. He is the memory of a collision. He is the scar that remembers. He exists in all 26 universes because he is the origin point. He is the center. The anchor. The constant.

He does not appear. He is felt. He is a presence. He is a mystery.

---

### The Heroes

Every universe has its heroes. Every hero has their story.

They are not perfect. They are broken. They are trying.

They fight. They lose. They get back up.

They are the heart. The mind. The weapon. The witness. The wildcard.

They are everything.

---

### The Villains

Every hero has their villain. Every villain has their reason.

They are not evil. They are not cruel. They are lost. They are broken. They are trying to survive.

They are mirrors. They are warnings. They are what the heroes could become.

They are everything the heroes fear.

---

### The Science

THE 26 is not magic. It is not fantasy. It is science we do not fully understand yet.

Phase transitions. Quantum entanglement. Block universe theory. Many-worlds interpretation. Quantum probability fields.

Everything is grounded. Everything has rules. Everything can be explained.

Even Jaxo.

### How to Contribute

**For Writers:**

1. Read the lore
2. Choose a universe
3. Write a story
4. Submit a Pull Request
5. Wait for review

**For Editors:**

1. Review Pull Requests
2. Check for mistakes
3. Request changes or approve
4. Pass to Sender

**For Senders:**

1. Review approved content
2. Merge the Pull Request
3. Publish to the site
